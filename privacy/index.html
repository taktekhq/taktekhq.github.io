Privacy

Taktek, LLC · last reviewed September 2026

This covers taktek.io and the sites Taktek, LLC operates. Some products collect more than this because of what they do. The privacy notice on the product site explains this. That notice is the specific answer.

What this site collects

taktek.io collects nothing about you. The site has no form, no login and no cookie set by Taktek, LLC. If analytics run, they record viewed pages, roughly which country from, and what linked here. The IP address is truncated before storage.

What the product sites collect

A product site selling something collects what Taktek, LLC needs to sell it. This includes a name, an email, an address and a phone number, plus whatever the product asks about your situation. Company formation sites ask what the company will do, where your clients are and roughly what you invoice. The answers decide whether the purchase is right for you.

Payments

Stripe takes payment on its own checkout. Taktek, LLC and Taktek sites never see or store card numbers. Stripe keeps a transaction record under its own privacy policy.

Bookings

Where a site offers a call, Cal.com handles the booking. Cal.com receives your name, your email and your chosen time.

Where somebody else does the work

Hobeich Legal performs the legal work on the company formation sites. What you send passes to the firm because the firm does the work. The firm holds a separate client file under lawyer confidentiality. Deleting data held by Taktek, LLC does not delete that file, and vice versa. Ask whichever one you mean.

Who else sees it

Stripe for payment, Cal.com for bookings, Google for analytics, and the third party performing the service where one exists. Public authorities where a product requires filing with them. Nobody else. Taktek, LLC sells nothing to anyone. The sites have no advertising networks.

How long it is kept

Payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Analytics for fourteen months. Anything a regulated third party must keep, such as a lawyer's client file, for as long as their rules say, which is usually longer.

What you can ask for

A copy of what is held, a correction, or deletion of anything not required to be kept. Say which product you mean because the answer differs.

Getting in touch

Write to nizar@taktek.io, or to the address on the product site if it concerns that product.
