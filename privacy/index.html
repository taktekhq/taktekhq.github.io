Privacy

Taktek, LLC · last reviewed September 2026

This covers taktek.io and the sites Taktek, LLC operates. Some products collect more than this, because of what they do. Where that is the case it is set out in that product's own privacy notice, on its own site, and that one is the specific answer.

What this site collects

taktek.io collects nothing about you. There is no form on it, no login and no cookie set by Taktek. If analytics are running, they record which pages were viewed, roughly which country from, and what linked here, with the IP address truncated before it is stored.

What the product sites collect

A product site that sells something collects what it needs to sell it: a name, an email, an address and a phone number, plus whatever the product asks about your situation. The company formation sites ask what the company will do, where your clients are and roughly what you invoice, because the answer decides whether the thing you are buying is right for you.

Payments

Payment is taken by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout. Card numbers are never seen or stored by Taktek or by any Taktek site. Stripe keeps its own record of the transaction under its own privacy policy.

Bookings

Where a site offers a call, the booking is handled by Cal.com, which receives your name, your email and the time you chose.

Where somebody else does the work

On the company formation sites the legal work is performed by Hobeich Legal. What you send is passed to the firm, necessarily, because the firm is the one doing it. The firm then holds its own client file, separately, under the confidentiality a lawyer owes a client. Deleting what Taktek holds does not delete that file, and vice versa. Ask whichever one you mean.

Who else sees it

Stripe for payment, Cal.com for bookings, Google for analytics, and the third party performing the service where there is one. Public authorities where a product requires filing with them. Nobody else. Nothing is sold to anyone, ever, and there is no advertising network on any of these sites.

How long it is kept

Payment records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Analytics for fourteen months. Anything a regulated third party is required to keep, such as a lawyer's client file, for as long as their rules say, which is usually longer.

What you can ask for

A copy of what is held, a correction, or deletion of anything not required to be kept. Say which product you mean, because the answer differs.

Getting in touch

Write to nizar@taktek.io, or to the address on the product site if it is about that product.
