One person reads this, so it gets a real answer rather than a ticket number. Write to taktekbot@gmail.com and expect a reply within one business day.

What to put in the email

Which product, what you were doing, and what happened instead. If it is about a payment, the date and the last four digits of the card are enough to find it. Screenshots help more than descriptions of screenshots.

If it is about a company formation

Anything about the legal work itself, the documents, the registry or the timeline is faster through Hobeich Legal directly, at gracia.hobeich@gmail.com. The firm is doing that work and has your file. Anything about the payment, the site or the intake comes here.

Refunds

Each product sets its own refund test and it is written in that product's terms. Say what you paid for and when, and you will get a straight answer about whether it is refundable rather than a form.

If you think a charge is wrong, write before filing a dispute with your bank. A dispute takes weeks, freezes the money while it runs, and nobody involved can fix the underlying problem during it. An email usually takes a day and can actually resolve the thing.

If something is broken

Say which device and which browser or app version. Bugs get fixed in the order they break things for people, so tell us what it stopped you doing.

Deleting your data

Ask here and say which product. What gets deleted and what has to be kept is in the privacy notice.

The rest

Terms and privacy cover what is promised and what is collected. Each product site has its own versions of both, covering that product specifically.
