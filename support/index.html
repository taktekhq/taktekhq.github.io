Support

Taktek, LLC · last reviewed September 2026

Write to nizar@taktek.io about any product. A person reads the email. You get a real answer instead of a ticket number. Expect a reply within one business day.

What to put in the email

Which product, what you were doing, and what happened instead. For a payment, the date and the last four digits of the card are enough. Screenshots help more than descriptions.

Refunds

Each product sets its own refund test. The test is in that product's terms. State what you paid for and when. You will get a straight answer about a refund instead of a form.

If you think a charge is wrong, write before filing a bank dispute. A dispute takes weeks and freezes the money. Nobody can fix the underlying problem during a dispute. An email usually takes a day and can resolve the issue.

If something is broken

Say which device and which browser or app version. Bugs get fixed in the order they break things. Tell Taktek, LLC what the bug stopped you doing.

Deleting your data

Ask at nizar@taktek.io and state the product. The privacy notice explains what gets deleted and what must be kept.

The rest

Terms and privacy cover what is promised and what is collected. Each product site has its own versions covering that product specifically.
