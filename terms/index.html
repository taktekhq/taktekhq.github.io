What you are buying

The product page and the checkout say what the payment covers, what it costs and what happens next. That description is the agreement. If something you were promised is not on the page or in the product terms, ask before paying rather than after.

Payment

Prices are shown in US dollars unless a page says otherwise. Payment is taken by Stripe. Taktek never sees or stores your card details. Where a payment is the first of two, the page says what the rest is and when it falls due.

Refunds

Each product sets its own refund test, because what counts as delivered differs from one to the next. That test is in the product's own terms, and it is the one that applies. Where a product sets none, anything not yet delivered is refundable on request.

Refunds go back to the card that paid. If you think something has gone wrong, write before filing a dispute with your bank. It is faster, and it is the only route where somebody can actually fix the thing.
